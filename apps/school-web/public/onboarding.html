Cancellation and data retention

If the school cancels or does not continue after the free trial, its portal access will end and its data will be retained securely for up to three months in case the school changes its mind. The school may request immediate permanent deletion at any time. Unless the school returns or requests earlier deletion, the data will be permanently deleted after the three-month recovery period.

Self-service activation is sent by email. Assisted requests are reviewed by our team.
