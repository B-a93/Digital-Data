COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How does the one-month free trial work?

The first 10 eligible schools receive guided onboarding and full portal access for one month. At the end of the trial, the school can accept its custom quote or stop without an automatic charge.

Does every school receive a separate system?

Schools use one secure multi-school platform. Each institution receives an isolated workspace, and one school cannot access another school’s records.

Can a school start with paper records?

Yes. Assisted onboarding and spreadsheet import help schools move existing information into the system in controlled stages.

Do schools need official email addresses?

No. Administrators and staff can use approved personal email addresses during onboarding.

Can it work on an Android phone?

Yes. The portal is mobile responsive and installable from a supported Android browser as a progressive web app.

Is school data publicly searchable?

No. Student records, fees, results and staff information remain behind secure authentication and are excluded from public search crawling.
